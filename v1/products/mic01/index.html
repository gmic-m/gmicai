MIC01 · AI Voice Device

Ultra-compact push-to-talk voice capture.

An ultra-lightweight wearable voice device for instant capture, AI interaction, and mobile-connected workflows. With a simple push-to-talk experience and all-day portability, MIC01 is an accessible hardware platform for teams building voice-enabled products and AI applications.

Simplicity meets AI.

MIC01 is built around one principle: capture voice instantly, with zero friction. Unlike larger recording devices, it focuses on portability, ease of use, and fast interaction — ideal for applications where users need quick voice input throughout the day.

/ Key Features

Everything you need — nothing you don't.

Push-to-Talk Operation

Capture ideas, commands, and conversations with a single press. A streamlined interaction model lets users focus on communication, not device management.

Ultra-Compact Design

Designed for everyday carry.

  • Lightweight construction
  • Pocket-sized form factor
  • Comfortable all-day wear
  • Minimal visual footprint

Bluetooth Connectivity

Connects seamlessly with mobile apps and AI platforms over Bluetooth 5.3 — reliable wireless communication with smartphones, tablets, and connected devices.

Instant Voice Capture

Record and transmit voice quickly for downstream processing.

  • Voice notes
  • AI assistants
  • Voice commands
  • Task capture
  • Workflow automation

Long Battery Life

Optimized for daily professional use. Despite its compact size, MIC01 delivers extended operation while staying lightweight.
