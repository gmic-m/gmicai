Industries — Public Safety

Public Safety Voice Capture.

Secure voice documentation hardware for public safety environments.

Learn More Start a Project
Challenges

What documentation has to get right.

Evidence documentation

Captured audio has to be complete, attributable, and dependable enough to stand as a record.

Security requirements

Sensitive recordings need controlled storage, secure transfer, and clear chain of custody.

Offline environments

Coverage isn't guaranteed in the field — devices must keep working without a connection.

Solutions

GMIC Public Safety Solutions.

Local storage

On-device storage so capture continues even with no network available.

Secure upload

Encrypted transfer to your systems when a trusted connection is available.

Private deployment

Deploy into your own controlled environment, not a shared cloud.

Why hardware

When software isn't enough.

Many healthcare AI companies start with mobile apps. As deployments scale, dedicated hardware becomes necessary.

Consistent user experience

The same reliable device behavior across every user and shift.

Improved adoption

Purpose-built devices people actually use day to day.

Better audio quality

Capture tuned for the real environment, not a generic mic.

Reduced workflow friction

Hardware that fits the task instead of interrupting it.

Enterprise deployment support

Provisioning, updates, and management built for scale.

Capabilities

Everything the device needs to do.

Tap any capability to expand.

Reliable pickup of speech across a room, not just inches from the mic.

Suppress background noise and remove echo so the recording stays clear.

Multi-mic arrays that focus on the active speaker and reject the rest.

Hands-free activation or deliberate trigger, depending on the use case.

Low-latency streaming of audio to and from your backend.

Clean and condition audio on the device before it's sent upstream.

Keep recording when there's no network and sync later.

Protected upload of captured audio to your systems.

Wi-Fi or cellular links with secure transport for deployed fleets.

Push firmware and configuration changes to devices in the field.

Onboard, configure, and monitor devices at scale.

Power profiles tuned for full-shift, all-day operation.
