The Problem

From AI Software to Real-World Devices.

Many AI companies have powerful software platforms but lack a practical way to capture data in real-world environments. GMIC helps organizations transform AI software into dedicated hardware products, including voice recorders, wearable microphones, smart badges, and enterprise voice devices. Our AI hardware implementation and custom AI hardware development services close the gap between a working model and a shippable device.
