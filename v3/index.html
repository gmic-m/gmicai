01 Why projects fail

AI doesn't fail on the idea. It fails on execution.

The execution gap

Turning AI into a real device is harder than it looks. Hardware engineering, firmware, acoustics, connectivity, certification, and supply chain — most AI teams don't have this in-house, and can't afford to build it from scratch.

Where projects stall

Stuck between prototype and production. Most AI hardware projects don't fail on the idea — they fail on iteration speed, manufacturing readiness, and the gap between a working sample and 10,000 units.

GMIC bridges the gap

GMIC bridges AI software and real-world deployment, moving you from prototype to scalable hardware — engineering, firmware, certification, and mass production, end to end.

Why not a generic ODM?

Own factory. No brokers. One accountable team from quote to delivery.

07 Our roots

From Gainstrong to GMIC.

GMIC is the AI hardware division of Gainstrong, a manufacturing company founded in 2009 and known for delivering high-volume consumer devices, IoT products, voice electronics, and communication hardware to global brands.

For more than a decade, Gainstrong built a reputation for engineering reliability, efficient production, and scaling hardware from prototype to millions of units. This foundation is what enables GMIC to move fast today — bringing AI companies from concept to production-ready device faster than any traditional ODM.
