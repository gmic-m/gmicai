Available Models

5 models, one card-size form factor

All five CS-series recorders share Bluetooth + Wi-Fi connectivity, bone-conduction microphones, and aluminum construction. Compare specs below to find the right fit.

HA-CS04

Slim — Ultra-thin Card Recorder

For apps and workflows that need a discreet, always-ready voice capture device. 3mm thin, 30g — slips into a pocket or magnetically mounts on a phone.

Recording
3-mic array (2 omni + 1 bone-conduction), one-touch record with mode switch
Connectivity
Bluetooth 5.3 (BLE) + Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz). Magnetic pogo-pin port for charging and audio export.
Battery
~25 hours recording. 400mAh, ~2h to full charge.

Sample includes

HA-CS04 device, MagSafe leather case, MagSafe ring, charging cable, user manual, and integration support guidance.

Pro — Card Recorder with Display
