How it works

Every visit becomes a coaching opportunity

Your sales platform turns recorded conversations into structured recaps with buyer needs, open promises, and next steps — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Structured recaps, generated by your platform

After each visit, the recording is uploaded to your server via Wi-Fi. Your sales platform processes the audio and generates a recap — buyer needs, objections, promises made, and recommended next steps.

Surface what matters. Buyer concerns, timeline, and decision-makers pulled from the conversation.
Catch open promises. When a rep commits to something but hasn't scheduled it, the recap flags it.
Coach with context. Managers review real conversations, not secondhand summaries.
